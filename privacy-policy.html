Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Before all the legalese, we wanted to say this: we have kids. We don't like all our information being traded around the internet, and we especially don't like information about our kids being traded around. It's written here below, but just know we only use the child information you give us to make and deliver their story. We don't use it or sell it for anything else.

Growing Minds LLC ("Growing Minds," "we," "us," or "our") operates the website growingminds.io. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our service.

1. Who We Are

Growing Minds LLC
Colorado, USA
Email: hello@growingminds.io

2. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly when filling out our story intake form:

  • Your email address
  • Your child's first name, age, and gender
  • Your child's ethnicity, if you choose to share it (used only so the illustrations look like your child)
  • Your child's physical description (hair color, length and style, eye color)
  • Your child's personality traits, interests, the milestone you want the story to focus on, and hometown (city and state)
  • Names of companions, siblings, or pets you choose to include
  • Any additional details you voluntarily provide in the customization field
  • Your shipping address (collected by Stripe at checkout)
  • Payment information (processed securely by Stripe — we never store card details)

We also collect technical data automatically when you visit our website, including your IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, the ad or link that brought you to us, and actions you take on the site (such as starting the story form or completing a purchase). We collect this through Google Analytics and the Meta Pixel, which use cookies and similar technologies. See Sections 7 and 8.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Generate your personalized story book
  • Email you your preview chapters, order confirmation, and shipping updates
  • Fulfill and ship your printed book order
  • Respond to customer service inquiries
  • Send you occasional updates about Growing Minds (you may unsubscribe at any time)
  • Improve our service through analytics
  • Measure how well our ads on Facebook and Instagram work, and show our ads to people who have visited our website (see Section 8)

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

Growing Minds is a service for parents and guardians. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under the age of 13. All information about children is provided by a parent or guardian on their behalf. By using our service, you confirm that you are at least 18 years old and are providing information about your child with full parental consent.

The information you provide about your child (name, age, appearance, interests) is used solely to generate their personalized story. We do not share this information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes, and we never send your child's name, age, appearance, or the other details you type about your child to Google Analytics, Meta, or any other advertising or analytics provider. Our analytics record only which story theme you chose (for example, "Making friends").

5. Data Sharing

We share your data only with service providers we use to operate our business:

  • Stripe — payment processing and shipping address collection
  • Resend — email delivery of your story
  • Lulu Direct — printing and shipping of physical books (receives your book file and shipping address)
  • Anthropic — AI story writing (receives the story details you enter in our form)
  • Google (Gemini) — AI illustration (receives your child's physical description and the story scenes to draw)
  • Vercel, Upstash, and Inngest — website hosting, storage of story drafts, illustrations, and book files, and background processing of orders
  • Airtable — internal customer record keeping (your email and your child's first name and age)
  • Google Analytics — website usage analytics
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook and Instagram) — advertising measurement and ad targeting, as described in Section 8

We do not sell your personal information for money. Our use of the Meta Pixel to show ads to people who have visited our website may be considered "sharing" for targeted advertising under some U.S. state laws. You can opt out of this at any time (see Section 8).

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address and order information for as long as necessary to fulfill your order and provide customer support. Working files created while we write and illustrate your story (drafts, outlines, and illustrations) are stored for up to 30 days, so that a preview can be upgraded to the full book and so we can resolve any problem with your order. Your finished book file is kept so we can print, reprint, or replace your book if needed. You may request deletion of your data, including your book file, at any time by emailing hello@growingminds.io.

7. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies from Google Analytics and Meta (the Meta Pixel) to understand how visitors use our website and to measure our advertising. These record information such as pages visited, time spent on the site, and actions such as starting the story form or completing a purchase. They do not record your child's name or the details you type about your child; they record only which story theme you chose (for example, "Making friends").

You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings. You can also opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

8. Advertising and Your Choices

We advertise on Facebook and Instagram. To do this, our website uses the Meta Pixel, a tool provided by Meta Platforms, Inc. When you visit our site, the Meta Pixel may share information with Meta, such as the pages you visit, actions you take (like starting the story form or completing a purchase), your IP address, and browser information. When you make a purchase, we may also send Meta a scrambled (hashed) version of your email address so Meta can match the purchase to an ad you saw. We never send Meta your child's name or any other details about your child.

Meta uses this information to tell us how our ads perform and to help us show ads to people who have visited our site, or who are similar to our customers. Meta's use of this information is governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.

You can limit this in any of these ways:

  • Opt out with us: email hello@growingminds.io with the subject "Do not sell or share," and we will stop sending any information we hold about you to Meta for advertising.
  • Adjust your Meta settings: visit your ad preferences in Meta's Accounts Center to control ads based on activity from other websites.
  • Use your browser: block or clear cookies, or turn on a privacy signal such as Global Privacy Control.
  • Industry opt-outs: visit optout.aboutads.info to opt out of interest-based advertising from participating companies.

9. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt out of marketing emails at any time
  • Opt out of the use of your information for targeted advertising (see Section 8)

To exercise any of these rights, email us at hello@growingminds.io.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure third-party payment processing via Stripe. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.
